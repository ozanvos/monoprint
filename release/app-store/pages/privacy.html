Monoprint privacy policy

Updated 5 October 2026

Monoprint creates code images locally on your Mac. You do not need an account to use the app.

Your code and files

Syntax highlighting, preview rendering and exports run on your device. Monoprint does not send your snippets, imported source files, custom fonts or exported images to us.

Saved drafts

The app saves your current snippet and settings locally so you can continue later. This draft stays on your device. Local files may be included in backups managed by you or your operating system.

Exports and clipboard

You choose where to save exported files. Copy and drag actions place the exported image on the system clipboard or pass it to the app you choose. The destination app and any sharing service you use have their own privacy practices.

Analytics and advertising

Monoprint does not include in-app analytics, advertising or tracking. Apple may provide App Store or operating-system diagnostics under the choices you make in your Apple settings.

This website

This website is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the service, Cloudflare processes request information such as your IP address, browser information and the page requested. We do not add website analytics or tracking cookies. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its practices.

Support

If you email contact@7ea.st, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message and attachments, to respond to your request. Please remove sensitive code before sending examples. You can contact us about access to or deletion of information you have sent.

Changes and questions

We will update this page if the app's privacy practices change. For questions, contact contact@7ea.st.
