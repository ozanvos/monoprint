Monoprint turns source code into images on your Mac. Paste code or open a file,
choose a language and theme, then export PNG, SVG or self-contained HTML.

• Light and dark themes, including editor palettes.
• Bundled coding fonts, custom TTF/OTF fonts, separate comment fonts and ligatures.
• Adjustable width, padding, filename and line numbers.
• Line and text highlights.
• Themed, solid, lattice, halftone and transparent backgrounds.
• PNG exports at 1× to 6×, clipboard copy and drag-out export.

Syntax highlighting, previews and exports run locally without an account or
internet connection. Your current snippet and settings are saved on your Mac.

Supports TypeScript, JavaScript, Rust, Python, Swift, Go, Hem and other
programming, markup and configuration languages.
