# Monoprint 0.2.1

This release adds motion to Studio and a quicker way to switch a theme's
appearance. It also ships the reworked Originals prepared for 0.2.0, which was
not published.

- Zoom presets glide the preview between Fit, 50% and 100%. Pinching, panning
  and resizing still follow the pointer exactly.
- Choosing a theme glides the selection ring to its tile. After a search or
  filter, tiles that move fade in in reading order.
- Clicking the selected theme again switches between its light and dark
  variants when the theme has both. The light and dark filters still pick the
  variant they show.
- Copy confirmations and the selected theme's check mark animate in, and the
  highlight toolbar fades in next to the selection.
- All motion follows the system Reduce Motion setting.

From 0.2.0:

- Add nine Originals: Anodized, Carbon Copy, Cyanotype, Greenbar, Index Card,
  Manila, Phosphor, Quadrangle and Vellum.
- Every one of the 38 Originals now offers both `--light` and `--dark`. With
  neither flag, Originals default to dark.
- Group the Studio theme picker into Print and paper, Office and stationery,
  Screens and signage, Objects, Annotation and Quiet frames. Searching a group
  name finds its themes. The library adds `Theme::group()` and
  `Theme::curated_rank()`.
- Fix long lines overflowing or being clipped in several frames.
- Remove decorative extras that competed with the code, such as fake status
  bars, menu bars, tape and corner marks.

**Breaking:** 19 overlapping or weak Originals are retired, and their IDs now
fail as unknown themes: ballast, carrier-sheet, column, contour, course,
departure-board, die-cut, gallery-recess, pendant, plumb, press-proof, quay,
quiet-typesetter, swatch-card, trace, traverse, two-colour-thermal,
ultraviolet-drum and well.

Desktop downloads support Apple Silicon macOS and Linux x64/ARM64. npm packages
support macOS arm64/x64, Linux glibc arm64/x64, and Windows x64. Windows desktop
downloads remain deferred.
